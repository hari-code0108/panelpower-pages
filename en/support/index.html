In-app purchases to support development

Support purchases are optional consumable in-app purchases. Whether a purchase is made and the amount paid do not change app functionality, and no items included in product names are provided. The same product can be purchased repeatedly.

On iOS, product price display, payments, purchase history and refunds are handled through the App Store. To check purchase history or request a refund, use Apple’s Report a Problem page.

On Android, in-app purchases are processed through Google Play’s billing system. Follow Google Play’s procedures to check purchase history or request a refund.

When contacting us about the purchase screen or completion processing
  • Device name and OS version
  • PanelPower app version
  • Date and time of the issue, selected product and displayed message

Do not include your Apple Account or Google account password, verification codes, payment methods, full purchase receipts or transaction identifiers in your email.

Account deletion

As a general rule, please delete your account from Settings in the PanelPower app. Identity verification through Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple is required before deletion.

If you need to transfer host privileges, deletion does not complete, you cannot sign in to the app or you encounter a similar issue, contact the email address above.

Information to include in your email
  • Subject: “PanelPower account deletion”
  • Your sign-in method (Google or Apple)
  • The situation or error message displayed

Do not include Google or Apple passwords, verification codes or Claim Codes in your email. Receiving an email does not automatically delete an account. We will provide guidance after verifying your identity and confirming the relevant account.

For details of data deleted and data not deleted, see Account deletion in the Privacy Policy.

Reporting a bug

Please provide as much of the following information as possible.

  • Device name and OS version
  • PanelPower app version
  • Date and time the issue occurred
  • Steps leading to the issue
  • Error message displayed
  • Frequency (every time, sometimes, once, etc.)
  • If needed, a screenshot with personal information hidden

If Room names, display names, records or similar information appear, hide unnecessary personal information before sending.

Responses

We generally respond within 3 business days. Additional time may be needed for investigations, identity verification, checks with external services or multiple data processing operations.

Spam filters and similar settings may prevent you from receiving our reply. Please allow email from panelpower.app@gmail.com.
